Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Who this covers

This policy covers the personal automation tools Robert Jimenez (@ItsRobbyAI) runs against his own Google account, including the "Youtube Automation" Google Cloud project. These tools are for personal use only and are not offered to the public.

What data the tools access

With the account owner's consent, the tools may read and send Gmail messages, read and write Google Drive files, and manage YouTube channel data for the owner's own account.

How the data is used

  • Data is used only to perform tasks the account owner asks for, such as replying to email or updating video details.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
