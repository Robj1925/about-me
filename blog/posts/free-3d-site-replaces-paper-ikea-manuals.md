1,223 IKEA products now have free 3D assembly guides that talk. If you ever got stuck on a paper manual, this is for you.

## What it is

The site is called Bygga. It is open source under the MIT license, it is free, and it needs no account. The source code is on GitHub, and the project was created on October 21, 2026.

## How it works

You type the name from the box, like KALLAX, MALM or BILLY, or the article number. Then:

- **Every step plays in 3D.** You drag the model to see any side.
- **Every screw gets a close up.** The step shows which part goes where.
- **Each step is read out loud.** A Voice button reads the step to you.

## An example: MALM

The MALM chest of 3 drawers is 33 steps. Step 12 of 33, for example, tells you it needs a 5 × 13 mm screw, two of them. Each step tells you which screw and how many, out loud, so you do not have to look back at a page.

## The catch

- **It is not from IKEA.** The site footer says it is not affiliated with any IKEA company. The guides come from a parametric engine, not from IKEA's own manuals.
- **The voice is English only.** More languages are on the roadmap.
- **Some shapes are missing.** Three cornered shelves, corner combinations and some PAX widths are not in yet.
- **The count varies.** The live site says 1,223 products. The README gives a lower number (1,171 products in 2,180 sizes).
