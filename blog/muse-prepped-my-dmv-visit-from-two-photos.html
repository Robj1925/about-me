Muse Prepped My Entire DMV Visit From Two Photos

I lost my car registration, so I sent Meta Muse photos of my documents. It found no open DMV appointments, then filled out the replacement form and built a walk-in checklist anyway.

✍️ By Robby J · 📅 November 10, 2026 · ⏱ 3 min read

The useful part of an AI agent is what it does after the first plan fails. My DMV errand shows it well: the appointment I asked for did not exist, and Meta Muse still sent me to the counter with everything done.

The problem

I lost my car registration and needed a replacement from the state DMV. Anyone who has been there knows the worst outcome is not the line. It is waiting in the line and then getting turned away at the window because one form or one document is missing.

The request

I photographed my car documents and gave Muse one short instruction: make me an appointment at the DMV, I lost my registration.

Dead end number one: no slots

Muse went to the state appointment portal and checked the calendar. There were no booking slots open. A simple assistant stops here and tells you to try again later. That is the moment where most "AI agent" demos quietly end.

What it did instead

Muse changed the plan from "book an appointment" to "be ready for a walk-in":

  • It got the right form. It downloaded the official replacement registration form.
  • It filled it out. It took the information from my document photos and completed the PDF.
  • It built a checklist. It listed the identification, the proofs and the fee amounts I needed to bring to the counter.

I supervised it the whole time, but I did not type a single field of that form.

The catch

  • AI cannot stand in line for you. With no appointments open, this was still a walk-in visit. What changed is the risk of a wasted trip, not the wait.
  • You hand over sensitive photos. Registration and identity documents carry a lot of personal data. Decide what you are comfortable sharing before you send them.
  • Check the form before you print it. An agent can fill a field with the wrong value just as fast as the right one. Read every line once.

Why this matters

This is the "keep going when blocked" behavior that Meta pitched as Active Focus. I covered the official demos in Meta's new AI remembers your habits and runs errands. Here it is on a real errand: the goal was "get my registration back", not "click the booking button", and Muse worked toward the goal.
