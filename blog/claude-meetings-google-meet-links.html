Claude Meetings for Google Meet: Links and Who Can Use It

✍️ By Robby J · 📅 November 14, 2026 · ⏱ 3 min read

Get the Claude Code skills and workflows I use to automate my content and my business in the AI Academy community on Skool.

Links

  • Official announcement (November 12, 2026): Claude Meetings
  • Help Center guide: Use Claude Meetings
  • Official demo video by Anthropic: Introducing Claude Meetings

Who can use it

  • Claude Meetings is a beta on Team and Enterprise plans only, in Claude and Claude Desktop.
  • It works with Google Meet only for now. Anthropic says other platforms are coming soon.
  • It is not available to organizations with zero data retention (ZDR), a HIPAA ready configuration, or customer managed encryption keys (CMEK).
  • On Team plans it is on by default, and an owner can turn it off.
  • On Enterprise plans, an owner turns it on for everyone or for some roles.
  • Owners manage it at Organization settings, then Products, then Claude Meetings (beta).

I am on the Max plan, so I cannot use it. My video uses Anthropic's official demo.

If you are on Team or Enterprise, the Help Center gives these steps:

  1. Go to Meetings in Claude.
  2. Connect your Google Calendar. An owner must turn on Google Calendar for your organization first.
  3. Select a meeting on your calendar.
  4. Click "Add Claude."

You can set auto join under Settings, then Capabilities, then Meetings.

How it works

  • Claude joins the call as a visible participant, with its own tile.
  • It answers questions out loud or in the chat. By default, it answers in writing.
  • It asks for your approval the first time it uses your connected tools. A yes applies going forward.
  • It builds docs, whiteboards and other artifacts while you talk, and teammates on the invite can edit them.
  • After the call, the notes, the action items and the transcript are on the meeting's page in claude.ai.
  • It saves a transcript, a summary and screen descriptions. It does not save audio.
  • It can stay in a call for up to eight hours, and it warns you before it leaves.

Watch out

  • Outside guests: on Team plans they are allowed by default. On Enterprise, an owner must allow them first. When outside guests are not allowed, Claude stays out if one is invited, and leaves if one joins partway through.
  • Make sure everyone on the call is OK with being transcribed before you add Claude.
  • When you ask it out loud, Claude asks each time to confirm who is speaking.
  • Beta pricing: notes and transcripts are free on Team and Enterprise, and artifacts are free through February 10, 2027. Answers that use connected tools count toward your plan usage.
  • Partner integrations (Gong, Attention, Granola, Fireflies.ai, Otter, Plaud) are coming soon. They are not live yet.

Sources: Anthropic announcement, Claude Help Center, official demo video. All checked November 14, 2026.
