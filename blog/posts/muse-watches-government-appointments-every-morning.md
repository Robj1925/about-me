The fastest way to get a government appointment is to stop refreshing the site yourself. I set Meta Muse to do it for me every morning.

## The problem: no openings

Appointments for things like a passport or a license can be booked out for months. The calendar I needed showed zero openings. Slots do come back when people cancel, but only someone who checks at the right time gets them.

## A daily check at 9:30 AM

I set Muse to log in to the booking portal and check it every day at 9:30 AM. It looks for newly released appointments and last minute cancellations. I do not open the site, and I do not keep a tab open.

## The alert comes to my phone

My desktop session stays paired with the Muse app on my phone. So when a slot opens, Muse pushes a notification right away, like this:

> "A slot just opened up for this Thursday. Do you want it?"

## Say no, and it keeps watching

This is the part that makes it hands off. If the time does not work for me, I say no. Muse takes that answer and runs the same check again the next morning at 9:30 AM. I do not have to set the task up again.

A one time search gives up when it finds nothing. A watcher that takes your answer and tries again tomorrow is what Meta means by Active Focus: the agent keeps working on the goal until it is done. I explained that idea in [Meta's new AI remembers your habits and runs errands](/blog/metas-new-ai-remembers-your-habits-and-runs-errands.html).

## The catch

- **Once a day is not every minute.** A slot that opens and fills between two checks is a slot Muse will not see. The 9:30 AM time is a choice, so pick a time that fits when your portal releases slots.
- **It logs in as you.** Muse needs access to your account on the portal. Be sure you are comfortable with that before you set it up.
- **You still make the booking choice.** Muse asks first. That is a good thing, but it means you have to answer quickly when the alert comes.

## The takeaway

Any task that is "check this page every day until something changes" is a good job for an agent. Government appointments are just the most painful example.
