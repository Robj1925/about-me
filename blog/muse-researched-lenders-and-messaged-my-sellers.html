Muse Researched Lenders and Messaged My Sellers

For a big purchase, I let Meta Muse compare loan terms, scan Marketplace listings and message sellers in English and Spanish. The key was one shared memory document that holds every quote.

✍️ By Robby J · 📅 November 9, 2026 · ⏱ 3 min read

A big purchase is not one decision. It is a loan, a listing and a seller, and each one lives in a different tab. I gave all three to Meta Muse and let it keep the whole deal in one place.

Step 1: the financing math

Muse compared interest rates, loan terms and the monthly payment for each option. That is the part most people do with a calculator and a spreadsheet, and the part where it is easiest to lose track of which lender said what.

Step 2: one memory document for the whole deal

This is the step that makes the rest work. Muse logged every lender offer and every seller's terms in one shared memory document. Quotes, specs and contact details all go into the same running file.

That document is why this is more than a search. When Muse writes to a seller or compares a new offer, it works from everything it already found, not from a blank chat. It is the same idea Meta sells as Active Memory, which I explained in Meta's new AI remembers your habits and runs errands.

Step 3: the listings

Next, Muse scanned Facebook Marketplace and other classifieds for listings that match the criteria. The matches go into the same document, next to the financing numbers.

Step 4: messaging sellers in two languages

Then Muse reached out to sellers directly to ask questions about condition and price. It wrote to them in English and in Spanish. A seller who answers faster in their own language is a seller you can actually close with.

The catch

  • Money decisions stay yours. Muse can compare the numbers, but check the rates and terms with the lender before you sign anything.
  • An agent that messages strangers speaks for you. Read what it sends, and set clear limits on what it can offer or agree to.
  • The memory document is only as good as what goes in it. If a quote changes, the document has to change too.

My take

The useful part here is not any single search. It is that one agent holds the loan math, the listings and the conversations together, so nothing gets lost between tabs.
