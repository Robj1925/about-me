The product video at the start of my short was not edited by anyone. There was no agency, no brief and no timeline. Somebody pasted a website address into a new tool called [Teasr](https://tryteasr.com/), and the tool did the rest. The video on Teasr's own homepage was made that way from teenagemanual.com.

Teasr is brand new. Its press kit says it launched in October 2026 and is based in Austria. Here is how it works.

## Step 1: paste your website and pick a length

You paste your address and choose 15, 30, 45 or 60 seconds. When I checked the page as a US visitor, pricing was:

- 15 seconds: $21.06
- 30 seconds: $31.86
- 45 seconds: $42.66
- 60 seconds: $53.46

Teasr's press kit lists base prices of €19.50 to €49.50, so the dollar figure you see depends on where you are. There is also a Studio plan at €49 a month for three videos at any length.

## Step 2: Teasr reads your website

Teasr says it uses "your words, colours, fonts and photos, taken from your website." On its homepage demo you can see what it found for the Teenage Manual site: the headline, the Geist font, four brand colours and the product photo.

The part I did not expect is the music. Teasr says every video "gets its own soundtrack, and the pictures move with it." So the cuts land on the beat instead of sitting on top of a stock track.

## Step 3: wait about 8 minutes

The homepage says the video is ready in about 8 minutes, and that you can close the tab because it emails you when it is done. The press kit and Teasr's llms.txt say about 10 minutes, so plan for somewhere in that range.

## Ask for changes in your own words

If the first version is not right, you describe the change in plain language. Teasr's own example is "Make the start punchier and end on the logo." Your first change is free, and Teasr says further changes cost less than a new video.

## What you get

Every video is 1080p at 60 fps and comes in both 16:9 and 9:16. That means one video works as a hero on your homepage and as a Short, Reel or TikTok. Teasr says the music is written for your video, so there is nothing to license.

## The agency comparison

Teasr's own comparison table puts an agency at $2,000 to $15,000 and 2 to 4 weeks for a 15 to 60 second video. In my short I said "months." That was my mistake: Teasr's table says weeks. Keep in mind this is Teasr's comparison, not an independent price survey. Its llms.txt is more balanced and says an agency is still better for bespoke brand films and live action.

## The limits

- **Motion graphics only.** Teasr's llms.txt says it makes motion graphics, not screen recordings of your app. If you need a product walkthrough, this is not that.
- **Your website is the input.** If your site has weak copy or poor product photos, the video will reflect that.
- **It is very new.** There is no API yet (Teasr says it is coming soon), and when I made the video I could not even find a Product Hunt page for it.

## My take

For a small product or a solo launch, the realistic alternative to Teasr is not an agency. It is no video at all. At around $21 for a 15 second cut in both formats, it is an easy experiment.
