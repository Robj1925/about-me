Claude voice mode now works in Apple CarPlay. Every drive can be a Claude chat with your hands on the wheel. But CarPlay is not the only upgrade in this update.

## How CarPlay works

Anthropic announced it in a thread on October 29, 2026: on iPhone, voice mode now works with Apple CarPlay. On the CarPlay screen you tap Claude, it says "Listening", and you just ask.

Voice also got better. It now answers sooner and hears you better in a noisy room.

## Multiple accounts

You might use this one more. On iOS and Android, you can now stay signed into more than one account and switch between them without signing out. That helps if you keep a work account and a personal account.

## 33 languages

The app now comes in 24 more languages, 33 in all, including Bengali, Danish, Swahili, Thai, Turkish, Ukrainian and Vietnamese. Note that these are the app's interface languages (the menus), not voice languages.

## The catch

- **CarPlay is voice only.** There are no files and no projects on the CarPlay screen.
- **It is not brand new.** CarPlay support was reported back in early September (Appleosophy, September 7). This update is Anthropic making it official.
- **Some things are not out yet.** Pull down search was reported as coming soon, so it is not part of this release.
