How Claude Wrote 90% of Anthropic's Codebase

Anthropic's S-1 filing says over 90% of the contributions to its codebase in September 2026 were written by Claude. Here is the exact wording and the numbers around it.

✍️ By Robby J · 📅 November 4, 2026 · ⏱ 2 min read

Claude wrote over 90% of Anthropic's code contributions in September 2026. That line is in Anthropic's IPO filing, so if you write code, you want the exact wording.

The exact line

Anthropic, PBC filed its Form S-1 on October 26, 2026. The filing says that over 90% of the contributions to its codebase in September 2026 were written by Claude.

Read that carefully. It is contributions, in one month. It is not every line Anthropic has ever shipped, and it is not "90% of all code".

Output per engineer

The next sentence in the filing says daily code volume per engineer was over 45 times the average daily volume in the year ended December 31, 2024.

The money

The same filing has the financials:

  • Revenue. Revenue for the first half of 2026 was $16.286 billion, up 1,300%.
  • One good quarter. The second quarter of 2026 shows an operating income of $974 million. That is for one quarter only.
  • A loss for the half year. Across the first six months of 2026, Anthropic still had a $693 million operating loss.
  • A large net loss. The net loss for the half year was $80.7 billion, mostly from the change in fair value of convertible notes, derivative liabilities and SAFEs.

What the filing does not say

The share price range on the cover is still blank. The filing went in on October 26, and the ticker on the cover is ANT, but there is no price range yet, so any valuation claim is a guess. Also, $974 million is one quarter of operating income. It is not a profit for the year.
