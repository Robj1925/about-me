Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Who this covers

This policy covers the personal automation tools Robert Jimenez (@ItsRobbyAI) runs against his own Google account, including the "Youtube Automation" Google Cloud project. These tools are for personal use only and are not offered to the public.

What data the tools access

With the account owner's consent, the tools may read and send Gmail messages, read and write Google Drive files, and manage YouTube channel data for the owner's own account.

How the data is used

  • Data is used only to perform tasks the account owner asks for, such as replying to email or updating video details.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Instagram comment replies (Robby Comment Replies)

The Meta app "Robby Comment Replies" answers comments on the @itsrobbyai Instagram account. When someone comments a keyword named in a video, such as STYLE, it sends that person one direct message with the link and replies to the comment.

  • Data received: the comment ID, comment text, the commenter's Instagram-scoped ID and username, and the post ID, as sent by Instagram's webhook.
  • Use: only to send the one reply and message that the commenter asked for, and to avoid answering the same comment twice.
  • Storage: comment IDs are kept for 14 days and a short activity log of the last 100 events is kept for troubleshooting. Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Data deletion

To delete any data about you, send a message through the contact section on the home page, or a direct message to @itsrobbyai on Instagram, with your Instagram username and the words "Data deletion". It is deleted within 30 days, and you get a confirmation. Stored comment IDs also expire on their own after 14 days.

Contact

Questions: use the contact section on the home page.
